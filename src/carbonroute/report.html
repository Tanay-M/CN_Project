What this experiment can say

  • 2005 traffic is paired with later European carbon by country, weekday and season. The two sources are not simultaneous measurements.
  • Carbon is a daily country-level consumption estimate, held constant within the day. There is no carbon predictor.
  • Capacities, propagation assumptions and six active demands define this experiment; it is not the full GÉANT backbone.
  • One-bin observation lag represents 15 scenario minutes. The reference paper campaign also tests immediate observations.
  • The contribution under test is the combination of prediction, shared capacity reservations and route-change safeguards. A positive result is not assumed.
